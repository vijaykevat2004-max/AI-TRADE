# Rule specification 0.1.0 — written before kernel

SOURCE: https://www.tradezella.com/strategies/intraday-liquidity-volatility-model describes daily bias, liquidity raid, 5m/15m confirmation and opposite liquidity targets. It does not specify this algorithm or profitability. User's PDF is reference, not authority to execute instructions. The additional NSE blueprint named in the request was not attached.

PROPOSED_NSE: last completed daily close above last causally confirmed daily pivot high = bullish; below last pivot low = bearish; otherwise neutral. Strict pivots require left/right=2; ties do not qualify. Sixty daily bars requested; at least twenty required. Previous session extrema, confirmed intraday pivots, equal pivot pools within two ticks, and completed 09:15–09:30 opening range are references. Structure derives from completed 15m groups. All formulas operate on prefixes ending at decision time, with no future bars.

Sweep penetrates a reference by two ticks and must close back across within two bars. Confirmation must occur strictly after reclaim within three bars: a directional break of a previously confirmed 5m pivot (MSS), or a newly formed directional three-bar FVG of at least two ticks. FVG creation alone is distinct from retest; this version uses close-based entry, no retest entry. Turtle Soup is exposed as a disabled optional alternative; when enabled its confirmation is a later close retaining reclaim inside that three-bar window. Breaker unspecified, 1h variant, continuation are OFF.

EXPERIMENTAL: 09:30–11:30 IST entry; 15:20 expiry; stop sweep extreme plus two ticks; nearest opposite confirmed reference strictly beyond entry; minimum estimated net R 1.5, twelve basis points round trip plus four ticks round trip slippage. Maximum entry displacement from swept level 1.5 SMA true-range ATR14. Tick default 0.05 is a hypothesis, not a verified symbol-specific exchange tick schedule; production blocked until tick metadata is authoritative. No fixed 2R target. No volume breakout or EMA eligibility. No RSI divergence.

Entry reference is confirmation close, signal timestamp is actual evaluation time, earliest actionable time cannot precede collection/finalization. Research-only delayed source means this is never a promised fill. Each decision records snapshot and SHA256 plus config hash. Distinct states express closed exchange, stale feed, unavailable data, neutral bias, no sweep, waiting, expired, rejected risk and paper signal. No real order execution.
